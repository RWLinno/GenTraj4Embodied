Question the score.

A reproducible audit of pose trajectories: timestamp-aware motion statistics, quaternion integrity, endpoint consistency, and cross-file overlap. No model training required.

Integrity findings

Only explicit checks are counted. Variable-length trajectories are allowed. A clean audit does not establish physical feasibility.

Motion, with time included

Speed and acceleration use actual time intervals. Units are coordinate-unit / timestamp-unit and coordinate-unit / timestamp-unit². These maxima describe discretized position trajectories, not robot joint limits.

Look at a trajectory

First eight readable records per source, in lexicographic group order. Position projections only; this is not a collision or orientation visualization.

Do these files support a multimodal claim?

Repeated endpoints are only a starting point. Group by the full task and scene, sample several outputs per condition, then measure valid coverage. Diversity across unrelated endpoints does not establish conditional multimodality.
